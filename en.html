Consistency check

  • The operating account was recalculated separately, line by line: revenue, ingredients, packaging, variable costs, staff, rent and overheads. It lands on the same EBITDA.
  • The 1,394,863 SAR of investment is the exact sum of the lines in the Investment tab.
  • The break-even of 159 tickets a day is recalculated two ways (formula and step-by-step search) and gives the same result.
  • The areas add up: 63 + 12 = 75 m² for the laboratory block, 20 + 55 = 75 m² for the counter and dining room, 150 m² in total, plus a 30 m² terrace.
  • The 52 seats (32 indoors, 20 on the terrace) give about 5 tickets per seat per day at 250 tickets, which is realistic with takeaway sales. At 400 tickets it rises to nearly 8 per seat: possible only with strong takeaway and a very busy location.

The conceptQuality coffee, a short and controlled offer

The menu stays simple: specialty coffee, all-butter viennoiserie, a few individual pastries and travel cakes packed as gifts or to take away. The laboratory produces each morning for the shop, then delivers to a few neighbouring hotels and cafés.

Staff enter through the changing room, then reach the laboratory past a hand basin; the delivery door opens into the stock room. On the right, the counter and bar (20 m²) connect to the laboratory through a 1.1 m passage. LaboratoryMachines from the cost library, not lump sums

The 63 m² laboratory uses references from the Bongard/BME library: two KRYSTAL 46.9V convection ovens with hoods and stands, an 80 L spiral mixer, a manual sheeter, a HERA-C proofer, a 20 L and a 60 L planetary mixer, a KRONOS-P blast chiller, five stainless steel tables, a marble table, three refrigerated cabinets, two freezer cabinets and ten trolleys. The blast chiller opens the way to semi-finished products and bases for B2B.

RisksWhat to confirm before committing

  • Ticket volume: validate the location and footfall with an on-site count.
  • The real rent of a 150 m² unit with a terrace in Medina, which varies strongly with the distance to the Haram.
  • Prices for the counter, espresso machine, signage and furniture: several quotes.
  • Equipment prices: confirm references and lead times with the supplier.
  • Licence type: bakery-pastry business with sales to the public.
  • Recruitment: 12 to 15 people, including a laboratory manager and an experienced barista.
  • The tax status of the partners (income tax or zakat), which changes net profit.
